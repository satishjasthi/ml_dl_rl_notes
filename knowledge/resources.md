# Resources

- [SimCLR paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05709) — primary source; indexed in [paper notes](../papers/simCLR/notes.md).
- [Google Research SimCLR repository](https://github.com/google-research/simclr) — official implementation; recorded in [paper resources](../papers/simCLR/resources.md).

- [SimCLRv2 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10029) — primary source; indexed in [paper notes](../papers/simCLRv2/notes.md).
- [NeurIPS SimCLRv2 abstract](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/hash/fcbc95ccdd551da181207c0c1400c655-Abstract.html) — published abstract and headline result; linked from [paper resources](../papers/simCLRv2/resources.md).

- [MAE paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.06377) — primary source; indexed in [MAE notes](../papers/masked_auto_encoders/notes.md).
- [Official MAE implementation](https://github.com/facebookresearch/mae) — author implementation; recorded in [MAE resources](../papers/masked_auto_encoders/resources.md).

- [DINO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.14294) — primary source; indexed in [DINO notes](../papers/dino-v1/notes.md).
- [Official DINO implementation](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dino/) — author implementation and pretrained-model resources; recorded in [DINO resources](../papers/dino-v1/resources.md).

- [DINOv2 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07193) — primary source; indexed in [DINOv2 notes](../papers/dino_v2/notes.md).
- [Official DINOv2 implementation](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2) — author implementation and pretrained resources; recorded in [DINOv2 resources](../papers/dino_v2/resources.md).

- [ViT paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929) — primary source; indexed in [ViT notes](../papers/vit_paper/notes.md).
- [ViT HTML paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.11929v2) — readable primary source for sections and equations; indexed in [ViT resources](../papers/vit_paper/resources.md).
- [ViT toy prototype](../papers/vit_paper/prototypes/toy_vit/README.md) — independent educational implementation linked from [ViT notes](../papers/vit_paper/notes.md).
